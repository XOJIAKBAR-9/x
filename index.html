About this journal

I started keeping this because I kept having thoughts in the shower that deserved better than to be forgotten by the time I found a towel.

There's no theme here on purpose — some entries are about work, some are about nothing in particular, and some are just questions I haven't answered yet. If something resonates, or you disagree entirely, I'd like to hear it.
